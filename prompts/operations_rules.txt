How to answer: a list of operations (Kundisch et al., 2022, Table 5). Use the ids of the taxonomy for existing elements.
- add_dimension: name, definition, application_rule (what to observe in an object and how to choose the characteristic), perspective, characteristics (each with name, definition and, for a closing characteristic such as "none", negation: true).
- add_characteristic: target [dimension id], name, definition, negation.
- rename: target [element id], with the new name, definition, application_rule or perspective (only the fields that change).
- swap: target [id, id] of two dimensions, or of two characteristics of one dimension.
- split: target [element id]; for a dimension, parts (at least two dimensions, as in add_dimension); for a characteristic, characteristics (at least two).
- merge: target [ids] of dimensions, or of characteristics of one dimension; name, definition (and for dimensions application_rule, perspective, characteristics).
- promote: target [characteristic id]; the new dimension's name, definition, application_rule, perspective and characteristics.
- demote: target [dimension id, id of the dimension that receives it]; name and definition of the resulting characteristic.
- delete: target [element id]; reason (no object, or redundant with another element). A negation characteristic is never deleted.

Cite in "evidence" the ids of the objects, concepts or open issues that justify each operation (e.g. "O3", "K12", "I4.2").

Rules checked by the program (a proposal that breaks them is returned to you):
- Every dimension belongs to exactly one perspective of: $perspectives.
- Every dimension has at least $min_characteristics_per_dimension characteristics and at most one negation characteristic.
- A negation characteristic names a stated absence of what the other characteristics describe (e.g. "No governance: immutable contracts"); it needs no object, and you do not delete it. No characteristic expresses missing information ("not stated", "unknown"): when the sources are silent, the cell is "no_information".
- Names are unique: no two dimensions, and no two characteristics anywhere in the taxonomy, share a name.
- (O9) Within a dimension the characteristics are mutually exclusive and collectively exhaustive: every object has exactly one of them. The program does not check this; you are responsible for it: where two characteristics could both apply, make the definitions or the application rule decide between them (e.g. precedence of the primary service).
- At the end, every perspective must have at least $min_dimensions_per_perspective dimensions; add them only when evidence supports them.
- Change an element only for a reason grounded in the evidence or in an open issue; return an empty list when no change is justified.
